Honest limits in the Tesla browser: GPS updates arrive roughly once every 30 seconds and positions are quantized, so speed, acceleration and g-force are derived from those sparse fixes — smooth in the simulator, steppy in the real car. GPS altitude is frequently null. The browser exposes no gyroscope and DeviceMotionEvent fires nothing here, so there is no true accelerometer feed. Nothing leaves this page: no map tiles, no weather calls, no server — units preference is the only thing saved, in localStorage.
